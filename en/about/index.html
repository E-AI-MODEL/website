About EAI

EAI is a way to examine how AI changes human action, learning and professional judgement. It was developed by Hans Visser, an educator and educational adviser in the Netherlands.

The question behind the model

A polished result does not tell us which decisions a person made along the way. EAI examines what people are learning or trying to achieve, which actions matter, and how AI changes those actions. the effect of AI.
