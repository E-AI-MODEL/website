Generative AI and second-language learning

Review of Zhang et al. (2026). Zhang and colleagues report positive average outcomes for generative AI in second-language education. This EAI review examines what supports those results and what still needs to be checked.

EAI · 14 November 2026 · 9 pages · English

Read the full review (PDF)Nederlands

What does the article report?

The meta-analysis combines 48 studies and 67 effect sizes. The overall estimate is Hedges’ g = 0.76, falling to g = 0.57 after three unusually large effects are removed. The studies differ substantially from one another (I² = 92.64%).

Which figures and studies need checking?

The 48 sample sizes in Table 2 sum to 4,378. The article reports 5,196 participants in its main text. The difference of 818 is not explained in the publication. Several included studies also raise questions about the selection criteria, study design classification and statistical reporting.

What can we conclude about learning?

The studies often report better immediate performance. Only four of the 48 studies include a delayed post-test. Evidence for retention, independent language use and transfer to new tasks is more limited.

This review does not independently recalculate all 67 effect sizes. The difference of 818 is a reporting discrepancy, not evidence that participants are missing.
