EAI · Education in the Loop

The work that
makes us human

Every profession involves decisions and actions that give the work its value. AI can speed up the result. Which parts of the work should people continue to own?

Why this matters

A teacher who leaves room for a student to reason. A mentor who makes a personal call rather than sending a template. A professional who takes responsibility for a difficult judgement.

AI can perform more of this work. The question is which actions people still need to learn, understand or decide for themselves.
